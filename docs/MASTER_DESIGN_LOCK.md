# Real Weather 365 — Master Design Lock

The user-provided Real Weather 365 concept image and `weather .pdf` are the visual acceptance references for this project.

## Locked home-screen language

- Edge-to-edge cinematic live environment.
- Compact centered location header with menu and overflow controls.
- Large lightweight temperature typography near the lower half of the scene.
- Weather condition, feels-like, high and low directly below temperature.
- One compact translucent hourly strip.
- Four compact glass metric cards.
- Five-item translucent bottom navigation.
- Dark blue-black glass surfaces with restrained cyan accents.
- Weather visuals remain the dominant layer; UI must not cover the scenery unnecessarily.

## Sunny / Clear Morning target

The Sunny screen is the first acceptance gate. It must evoke the reference composition: bright blue sky, warm sun glow from the upper-left area, layered snow mountains, dense green pine banks, a clear reflective lake, subtle clouds and restrained motion.

No other weather environment is considered visually approved until the Sunny reference-match screen is reviewed first.
